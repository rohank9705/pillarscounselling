Types of Therapy

Choosing therapy can feel overwhelming, especially if you are unsure which approach may be right for you. Different therapeutic approaches offer different ways of understanding experiences, creating change, and supporting personal growth.

At Pillars Counselling Services, my main therapeutic approach is Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT). However, I recognise that therapy is personal, and the most suitable approach will depend on your individual needs, preferences, and goals. On this page is an overview of some common therapeutic approaches to help you explore the different options available.

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)

CBT is a structured, goal-focused approach recommended for anxiety, depression, stress, and more. It explores how our thoughts, feelings, and behaviours influence each other. Therapy helps you recognise unhelpful patterns, develop new perspectives, and learn practical skills to respond to life's challenges in healthier ways. Read more

Person-Centred Therapy

This approach provides a safe, non-judgemental space to explore your experiences at your own pace. Based on the belief that everyone has the capacity for positive change, the therapist offers genuine acceptance and support. Rather than directing outcomes, therapy helps you build self-awareness and become a more fulfilled version of yourself. Read more

Psychodynamic Therapy

Psychodynamic Therapy explores how past experiences and deeper emotional patterns influence how you experience life today. By looking at both conscious and unconscious processes, therapy helps you make sense of recurring relationship or behavioural patterns, creating opportunities for meaningful change. Read more

Other Therapeutic Approaches

Family Therapy

Family Therapy offers a supportive, non-judgemental space to explore communication and relationship dynamics within the family system. It helps families navigate conflict, major life changes, or individual challenges together. Sessions are flexible and can involve the whole family, couples, or anyone who plays a significant role in your life. Read more

Humanistic Therapy

Humanistic Therapy focuses on the whole person, emphasizing self-awareness, personal growth, and reaching your potential. In a highly accepting and supportive environment, you are encouraged to explore your values and emotions without judgement. This approach empowers you to recognise your strengths and take an active role in shaping your life. Read more

Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing (EMDR)

Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing (EMDR) is highly effective for trauma and PTSD. It is based on the idea that distressing experiences can remain inadequately processed. Using structured procedures like guided eye movements, EMDR helps the brain process these difficult memories, reducing their emotional intensity and disruptiveness. Read more

Existential Therapy

Drawing on philosophy and psychology, this approach explores deep human challenges like identity, meaning, choice, and purpose. Rather than simply removing symptoms, Existential Therapy helps you explore what truly matters to you and how you want to live. It is especially helpful during periods of transition, uncertainty, or deep questioning. Read more
