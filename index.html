Pillars Counselling Services is a new and growing therapeutic service, created with the aim of providing accessible, compassionate support for individuals navigating life’s challenges. Although still in its infancy, this website and service are developing with a commitment to creating a safe, supportive space where people can explore their thoughts, feelings, and experiences at their own pace.

OUR METHODS

My main therapeutic approach is Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), which focuses on understanding the connection between our thoughts, emotions, and behaviours, while supporting individuals to develop new perspectives and practical strategies for positive change. However, I recognise that therapy is not a one-size-fits-all approach. Everyone’s experiences, needs, and preferences are different, and the right therapeutic relationship and approach are essential. Through this service, I aim to help guide individuals towards the type of therapy that feels most suitable for them, whether that is CBT or another therapeutic modality that better aligns with their goals.

WANT TO KNOW MORE?

At Pillars Counselling Services, the focus is on offering support, understanding, and a pathway towards greater self-awareness, resilience, and wellbeing. Whether you are seeking help during a difficult period, looking to understand yourself better, or considering therapy for the first time, you are welcome to explore the support available.

Get in contact with us and we can talk more about what suits you best.
