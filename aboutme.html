About Me

Creating a safe, non-judgmental space for your healing journey.

Hello, and welcome. My name is Daryl. I was born in Glasgow, Scotland, and have a good understanding of the unique pressures and challenges that many people in this part of the world experience. Growing up here has given me an appreciation of the resilience people show, even when life feels overwhelming. Before becoming a Cognitive Behavioural Therapist, I spent over 20 years working in a variety of roles. In my twenties, I worked across the environmental sector, with roles in horticulture, habitat restoration and with recycling charities, before spending much of my thirties in retail management across Newcastle and Ireland. These experiences allowed me to meet people from all walks of life and reinforced my interest in understanding what helps people overcome adversity and thrive.

Today, I am a qualified Cognitive Behavioural Therapist, having completed my CBT Diploma with ACT Counselling Services. I am a registered member of both the British Association for Counselling and Psychotherapy (BACP) and COSCA, and I am currently studying towards a BSc (Hons) in Psychology. I believe therapy is built on trust, collaboration and compassion. Whether you're experiencing anxiety, low mood, stress or simply feeling stuck, I aim to provide a warm, supportive and non-judgemental space where we can explore your experiences, develop practical coping strategies, and build strong foundations for your wellbeing.

Training & Core Competencies

  • Specialised Support for Anxiety, Depression, Stress & Self-Esteem
  • Evidence-Based CBT Techniques (Cognitive Restructuring, Behavioural Activation & Exposure)
  • Inclusive Therapy for Diverse & Neurodivergent Populations
  • ASIST Suicide Prevention & Crisis Safety Planning
  • Rigorous Safeguarding, Confidentiality & Ethical Practice
